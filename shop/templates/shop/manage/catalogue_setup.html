The configuration behind the catalogue: what a listing is made from, and how designs are grouped into collections.

Product templates

New template

A template is a reusable product setup: the garment, its nominal costs and its default sizes. Every file you drop on the Listings page becomes a draft listing per template (the first tee template and the first sticker template are used). Editing a template changes new uploads only; existing listings keep their own values. Suggested price uses the pricing rule (base + ship) × 2.5, rounded to the dollar.
